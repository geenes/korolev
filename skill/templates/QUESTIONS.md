# Owner questions

Copy the message below and replace the text in brackets. Repeat the numbered block for each decision, up to five per message. Update the last line to show how to answer all the questions in one reply.

```text
1. [What do we need to decide?]
[Why does this matter to users, money or time?]

a) [What will happen if you pick this?] (Recommended)
[One line on why we recommend this.]
b) [What will happen if you pick this?]

Reply to this message with 1a or 1b.
```

## Worked example

```text
1. How much should the app spend each day making short summaries of people's notes?
Each summary costs us money, and people have to wait when we reach the daily limit.

a) $20 a day. At $20, the app pauses summaries until the next day. (Recommended)
This keeps costs down while we learn how often people use summaries.
b) $50 a day. At $50, the app pauses summaries until the next day. The app can make more summaries each day.

Reply to this message with 1a or 1b.
```
